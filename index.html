Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

Welcome to Nziyo Hymnal. Your privacy is a priority for us. This policy outlines how we handle data within the Nziyo Hymnal mobile application to ensure your worship experience remains entirely your own.

1. Local Data Collection & Features

To provide a fast, seamless, and private offline worship experience, Nziyo Hymnal processes and stores data strictly locally on your mobile device. We do not run external servers to collect this information.

  • User-Generated Content: Any personal notes, prayers, or sermon reflections you type within the app are saved directly to your device's internal storage.
  • App Preferences: Your customized layout settings, including preferred language, font sizing, and theme preferences (e.g., Light or Dark mode) are retained locally.
  • Usage Data: The specific hymns you mark as favorites or compile into custom lists are kept entirely within your device's local database.

2. In-Transit Data & Third-Party Services

While we believe in a localized approach to your spiritual reflections, certain advanced, interactive features require secure data transmission to trusted third-party services:

  • Google AI (Gemini API): When you utilize AI-powered features—such as generating devotional reflections, explaining deep Shona or Ndebele liturgical metaphors, mapping scriptures, or utilizing Semantic Natural Language Search—the app securely transmits only the relevant hymn lyrics or your explicit search query to Google's servers. No personal notes, identifying details, or user profile information are ever shared. This text is processed on-the-fly to return your result in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Social Sharing (Lyric Cards): When you choose to generate a "Lyric Card," the image is compiled locally on your phone and passed to your device's native system sharing menu. Nziyo Hymnal does not track, log, or see who you send these cards to or what platform you select.

3. Data Storage & Deletion

Because your data is decentralized and sits completely on your physical hardware, control over its preservation rests entirely with you:

  • Permanent Deletion: If you clear the app's cache/data in your device settings or completely uninstall Nziyo Hymnal, all your local data—including your written notes and favorites—will be permanently deleted. Because we do not store this on an external cloud server, we cannot recover this data for you.
  • Device Backups: Your local application data may be backed up securely if you have system-wide cloud backups enabled on your device (such as Apple iCloud or Google One Backup).

4. Data Security & Children's Privacy

We are committed to maintaining the integrity of your private space:

  • Security: Since your personal devotional notes reside on your phone, their security relies on your hands. We highly recommend utilizing device-level security protocols—such as alphanumeric passcodes, fingerprint readers, or facial recognition—to protect your device from unauthorized physical access.
  • Children's Privacy: Nziyo Hymnal is completely safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly target, collect, or solicit data from children. While the core app is a safe, static tool for worship, parental guidance is advised when children use the interactive AI search and reflection features.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding your privacy while using Nziyo Hymnal, please contact us through the official project repository or the developer contact information provided on the app listing.
